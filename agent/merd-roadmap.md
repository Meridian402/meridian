# What's next: Merd's forward feed

The mirror of merd-shipped.md, pointed at the future. This is what Merd may say
is COMING, so the daily plan can carry the project's trajectory, not just what
already shipped.

## The rule for a line here

Say what will become **true for a user**, plainly, with **no date and no hard
promise**. "Coming" and "next" are allowed; "soon", "this week", "any day" are
not. Never describe how it is built, never imply anything is currently broken or
missing, and never commit to something that is not genuinely being worked toward.
If it is not real enough to stake the account's credibility on, it does not go
here. When something ships, move its line to merd-shipped.md.
